Our tower's antenna was last serviced around 2012, and its elements are now damaged,
which keeps our radios from reaching their full potential. We're planning to replace it,
likely with a new tower and an upgraded antenna.

We also plan to add a [SatNOGS](https://satnogs.org/){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"}
satellite ground station, which would let us receive and share data from satellites as
they pass over Clemson.

We'll post updates here as the project moves along.
