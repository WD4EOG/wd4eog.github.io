[MeshCore](https://meshcore.io){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"} lets you send text messages with
small, low-cost LoRa radios instead of cell towers or the internet. It keeps working when cell
service and Wi-Fi are down, and it doesn't need a license at the default power settings.

We put together a free beginner's guide for anyone who wants to try it, whether or not you
have any radio experience. Every radio term is explained in plain English, and there's a glossary
at the back.

## What's inside

1. **Meet MeshCore:** what it is, radio basics, how a mesh network works, and the kinds of nodes.
2. **Get set up:** what to buy, installing the app, loading the firmware, pairing your phone, and
   choosing the USA radio preset.
3. **Start messaging:** the Public channel, adding contacts, private messages, channels, and room servers.
4. **Range and repeaters:** tips for better signal, checking your signal, our local mesh, and setting up a repeater.
5. **Help and reference:** troubleshooting, a glossary, and useful links.

The links in the PDF are clickable, so it works on a phone or laptop as well as on paper.

## Our local mesh

The club's MeshCore repeater and the Anderson Radio Club's repeater are both on the roof of
Byrnes Hall. If your radio can reach either one, your messages can travel much farther than your
radio could manage alone.

Have questions, or want help flashing and pairing a radio? Bring it to a club meeting and we'll
help you get on the air.
